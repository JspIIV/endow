A grant is usually decided behind closed doors, and the criteria bend to the choice. Endow fixes the criteria first, in public, and lets the work decide.

open_grant(title, criteria, reward): a funder posts plain-words criteria and escrows a reward, bound to the caller. apply(grant_id, delivery_url): an applicant names one public page as their delivery; a funder cannot apply to their own grant. review(grant_id, app_index), open to anybody, fetches that delivery; a round reads it against the fixed criteria: MET, UNMET or UNCLEAR. The first delivery judged MET, while the grant is open, is awarded the reward and the grant closes; UNMET rejects it, UNCLEAR leaves it open. The evidence is the applicant's own delivery against criteria fixed before any application arrived, so the bar cannot be bent to a favourite. An unreadable or not-found delivery is UNCLEAR, never a win. award(grant_id) and balance(address) let a payout contract act on it. Proven on Asimov; tests in tests/endow_rules.py.

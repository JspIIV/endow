Grants are usually decided behind closed doors, criteria bending to the choice. Endow fixes the criteria first, in public, and backs the reward with real value.

open_grant(title, criteria) is payable: a funder sends the reward as native value, held in escrow; the reward equals the value sent. apply(grant_id, delivery_url) names a public delivery page; a funder cannot apply to their own grant. review(grant_id, app_index), open to anybody, fetches it; a round reads it against the fixed criteria: MET, UNMET or UNCLEAR. The first MET, while open, pays the escrow to that applicant by native transfer, exactly once, and closes; UNMET rejects it, UNCLEAR leaves it open. cancel(grant_id) lets the funder reclaim an open grant with no applications; an invalid open refunds the value.

The reward path is real: opening debits the funder, the escrow is held, the winner is paid once. Proven on GenLayer Studio where value moves; same source on Asimov. Tests in tests/endow_rules.py.
